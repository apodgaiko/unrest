# v0.4.5 independent integrated validation amendment

Status: maintainer approved 2026-09-28. This changes the release evidence floor;
it does not approve the candidate, tag, installation, or publication.

The earlier correctness-release profile remains historical authority for its
scope and decisions. Its `correctness`, `runner`, and `ruler` gates are
superseded because the temporary 52-case validator source baseline is
incomplete and rebuilding that platform would delay the release. The five
accepted product returns, referenced evidence and component commits have since
been recovered and independently rechecked; this correction does not restore
the missing external validator source. The superseded gates are neither passed
nor silently deferred. The original 52 cases and historical assertions must never be reported as executed
or passed by the replacement gate.

The replacement gate requires fresh independent, candidate-bound integrated
validation with an explicit disposition for **every one of the 70 contract
targets**. The 67 product targets (INQ 9, EVD 8, LEG 16, ACP 10, ACT 19,
and CROSS 5 other than CROSS005) receive pass, fail, or blocked verdicts.
EVAL001, EVAL002, and CROSS005 receive verdicts against the qualified
replacement obligations below, with their superseded external duties recorded
as superseded, never passed. Validators must exercise the actual
Python library, CLI, MCP, background Mission, persistence/restart, and installed
wheel surfaces wherever the target has such a surface. Tests and source review
support the verdicts but cannot replace real-surface proof. The validation
matrix must cover ordinary behavior and the relevant refusals, stale evidence,
privacy, authority, cancellation, recovery, and compatibility paths across
Inquiry, current evidence, legibility/supervision, ACP launch, and activation
adapters. Independent validators may reuse one fresh real-surface capture for
multiple targets only with explicit target-specific reasoning and evidence.

The other 14 gates of the approved profile remain required, including slice
dispositions, canonical integration, independent functional verdicts, bounded
live Inquiry with named planner consumption, real Python 3.13 process behavior,
exact archive and installed lifecycle, all 15 extracted persistence cases,
recursive checks, a single full source-suite checkpoint on the final frozen
candidate, fresh planning review, successful candidate-bound CI, and blocker
reconciliation. The replacement gate and functional gate may share evidence;
neither may mark an unexercised target passed.

## Qualified governance contracts

EVAL001 now requires an independent validator to own a closed case inventory,
candidate identity, expected outcomes, evidence paths, and per-target verdicts.
It must run without product-controlled oracles, never ship validator material in
the wheel, and never certify a benchmark. EVAL002 now requires that validator
to keep sensitive live inputs and expected outcomes outside product artifacts,
record safe digests and source custody, and prevent private bodies from entering
the release packet. CROSS005 now requires a five-slice disposition ledger,
independent target verdicts on the exact integrated candidate, blocker
reconciliation, and a separate maintainer release decision. The original
external runner, protected-bundle, 52-case and M-CERT duties of these three
contracts are superseded for this release only. No historical external return
may be cited as current candidate evidence without exact source reconciliation.

The closed case inventory and target-to-case map must exist before validation.
Each case records its real surface, setup, expected observation, refusal or
recovery oracle where relevant, exact candidate identity, evidence location,
and pass/fail/blocked result. Every target verdict names its supporting cases;
one capture can serve several targets only when each distinct promise has an
observable assertion. ACT006 must enumerate all 27 applicable scenarios from
`measurement/correctness-release-applicability.v1.json`, including the 18
Python/adapter and nine installed-wheel counterparts. Its nine deferred CLI
outcome scenarios remain deferred without pass or execution credit. ACT008
requires a genuinely submitted matching DAG; ACT009 requires a real candidate
lease, admitted candidate, campaign freeze, and external decision owner.

Release reporting must retain `benchmark_certified: false` and
`improvement_claims: []`. A failure or missing required observation blocks the
affected candidate. After all evidence is complete, the maintainer still makes
a separate release decision before tagging, publishing, or changing any
installation.
